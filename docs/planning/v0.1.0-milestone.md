# v0.1.0 milestone and issue plan

- Milestone: `v0.1.0 — first public baseline`
- Status: draft; issues are not yet created on GitHub because repository ownership and authentication are not confirmed.
- Rule: do not create duplicate issues. When the remote is available, create these once and link them to this document.

## Milestone goal

A public, installable baseline that demonstrates the library across the three initial domains and the Beijing adaptation profile, with documented standards, validation, and a reproducible sample.

## Definition of done

- [ ] A1 national baseline layout template with Chinese title block.
- [ ] Initial land-use, zoning, three-zones, road, water, settlement, and facility styles.
- [ ] Village/town, territorial spatial, and heritage domain packs.
- [ ] Beijing adaptation profile documented.
- [ ] At least one complete sample sheet per domain pack.
- [ ] Install and validation scripts.
- [ ] Quick start, field mapping, standards mapping, licence status, and release notes.
- [ ] Tested on declared QGIS versions.
- [ ] Release manifest and checksums.

## Issues

### Issue 1 — Define project scope and Beijing adaptation basis

**Goal:** Record what the library covers, what it excludes, and which Beijing standards and local conventions inform the profile.

**Deliverables:**
- `docs/scope.md` (baseline exists)
- `docs/profiles/beijing.md`
- A list of Beijing-specific fields, symbols, sheet conventions, and deviations from the national baseline.

**Dependencies:** standards baseline.

**Acceptance:**
- National baseline and Beijing profile are clearly separated.
- Each Beijing rule has a source, edition, and clause/page where available.
- The profile does not claim national applicability.

**Out of scope:** Other provincial profiles.

### Issue 2 — Define field conventions and data adaptation

**Goal:** Define the minimum attribute fields required by styles and templates, and how source data is mapped to them.

**Deliverables:**
- `docs/field-mapping.md`
- A small CSV schema for land-use, zoning, roads, facilities, and heritage layers.
- Rules for missing values, unknown classes, and local aliases.

**Dependencies:** Issue 1.

**Acceptance:**
- Every style that depends on a field names the field, type, and allowed values.
- A sample CSV validates against the schema.
- No personal or restricted data is committed.

**Out of scope:** A full statutory database schema.

### Issue 3 — Build the minimal complete sheet sample

**Goal:** Produce one complete A1 sheet that exercises the title block, map frame, legend, north arrow, scale bar, source note, and a representative map.

**Deliverables:**
- One `.qpt` template.
- One sample project and one exported PDF/PNG preview.
- A short verification note.

**Dependencies:** Issues 1 and 2.

**Acceptance:**
- Page size, margins, title-block fields, and legend are readable at A1.
- Chinese text renders with the documented font fallback.
- No absolute paths or missing SVGs.
- A reviewer can reproduce the sheet from the repository.

**Out of scope:** All domain packs.

### Issue 4 — Village and town planning pack

**Goal:** Provide styles and symbols for village classification, settlement system, land layout, facilities, roads, and industry.

**Deliverables:**
- QML styles and SVG symbols.
- A sample sheet.
- Documentation of fields and standards.

**Dependencies:** Issues 2 and 3.

**Acceptance:**
- At least one complete village/town sheet.
- Symbols are distinguishable in print and screen.
- Local profile differences are documented.

**Out of scope:** A full village planning data model.

### Issue 5 — Territorial spatial planning pack

**Goal:** Provide styles for three zones and three lines, planning zones, land-use classification, transport, ecology, and major facilities.

**Deliverables:**
- QML styles, colour ramps, and SVG symbols.
- A sample sheet.
- A standards mapping table.

**Dependencies:** Issues 2 and 3.

**Acceptance:**
- At least one complete territorial spatial sheet.
- Colours and symbols are traceable to a public standard or a recorded approximation.
- Draft-standard language is used correctly.

**Out of scope:** All provincial variations.

### Issue 6 — Heritage conservation pack

**Goal:** Provide styles for historic towns/villages, protected areas, buffer zones, historic buildings, historic environment, character zones, and heritage routes.

**Deliverables:**
- QML styles and SVG symbols.
- A sample sheet.
- Documentation of conservation terminology and fields.

**Dependencies:** Issues 2 and 3.

**Acceptance:**
- At least one complete heritage sheet.
- Protection boundaries and buffer zones are visually distinct.
- The pack does not imply legal designation without a source.

**Out of scope:** A heritage inventory database.

### Issue 7 — Layout and Chinese typography

**Goal:** Define A0–A3 sheet templates, title-block fields, font fallback, legend layout, north arrow, scale bar, and sheet numbering.

**Deliverables:**
- `.qpt` templates.
- A typography and layout guide.
- A font fallback table.

**Dependencies:** Issue 3.

**Acceptance:**
- Templates open in the declared QGIS versions.
- Chinese text does not overflow the title block.
- Fonts are open-source or documented fallbacks.
- A1 and A3 at minimum are tested.

**Out of scope:** A full brand system for a specific institute.

### Issue 8 — Load, render, export, and compatibility validation

**Goal:** Provide scripts and checks for loading resources, rendering samples, exporting PDFs, and detecting compatibility problems.

**Deliverables:**
- `tools/validate.py`
- `tools/export_pdf.py`
- A compatibility matrix for QGIS 3.40 LTR and 4.x.

**Dependencies:** Issues 3–7.

**Acceptance:**
- Validation catches missing fonts, missing SVGs, absolute paths, invalid XML, and wrong page sizes.
- A clean checkout can reproduce the sample exports.
- Known version differences are documented.

**Out of scope:** A full CI matrix for every operating system.

### Issue 9 — Documentation, licence, and release packaging

**Goal:** Prepare the public v0.1.0 release.

**Deliverables:**
- Quick start, field mapping, standards mapping, known issues.
- Final licence decision and notices.
- Manifest, checksums, release notes, and tag.

**Dependencies:** Issues 1–8.

**Acceptance:**
- A new user can install and open a template in under 15 minutes.
- Licence status is explicit; no unresolved asset is bundled.
- Release assets include manifest and checksums.
- No credentials, personal data, restricted data, or proprietary templates.

**Out of scope:** v1.0.0 and a plugin GUI.
