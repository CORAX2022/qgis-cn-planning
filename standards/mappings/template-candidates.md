# First-template candidates

No template is selected in this round. These are candidates for a later依据卡 and user decision. Paper size, orientation, and information layout are deliberately not fixed.

## Candidate 1 — Beijing township territorial spatial master plan sheet

- **Purpose:** A township-level plan sheet for Beijing, closest to the township/village planning use case.
- **Basis completeness:** Partial.
  - `BJ-DB11-1949-2021` is registered but the text is not yet obtained or read.
  - CJJ/T 97-2003 supplies general drawing rules.
  - SD-MAP-2019 supplies a worked provincial layout logic, but it is a draft and not Beijing.
- **Known requirements:** figure title, map boundary, north arrow/wind rose, scale, legend, signature, date, sheet ordering, text-height ranges, paper-family values from CJJ.
- **Still to decide:** sheet size (A1/A0/other), orientation, single-sheet vs multi-sheet set, title-block field layout, information zones, map scale, legend position, and whether the first sheet is a status map or a planning-structure map.
- **Why consider it first:** it is the closest to the Beijing township/village scope and can serve as the first local profile. It should not start until the Beijing township standard is obtained and read.

## Candidate 2 — Beijing territorial spatial zoning plan sheet

- **Purpose:** A district-level zoning plan sheet under the Beijing zoning-planning standard.
- **Basis completeness:** Partial, weaker than Candidate 1.
  - `BJ-DB11-1947-2021` is registered but the text is not yet obtained or read.
  - General rules from CJJ and the Shandong guide apply only by analogy.
- **Known requirements:** general figure title, border, north arrow, scale, legend, signature, date.
- **Still to decide:** zoning classification and colour basis, sheet size, orientation, title-block layout, legend order, and the relationship to the current land-use classification.
- **Why consider it:** it is a core Beijing planning product, but the zoning classification and colour rules are not yet extracted.

## Candidate 3 — historic building / historic district conservation sheet

- **Purpose:** A heritage conservation sheet for a historic district, historic building group, or traditional village.
- **Basis completeness:** Weakest of the three.
  - The Beijing historic-city regulation is a legal/terminology source, not a drawing standard.
  - `GB/T 50357-2018` is registered but not read.
  - Protection boundaries, buffer zones, and building categories need separate legal and technical sources.
- **Known requirements:** general drawing elements from CJJ; heritage terminology from the Beijing regulation.
- **Still to decide:** protection-object type, legal boundary source, classification of buildings, sheet size, orientation, title-block layout, and whether the sheet is a inventory, a protection framework, or a design guidance sheet.
- **Why consider it:** heritage is an explicit project domain, but the evidence base is not ready for a first template.

## Recommended sequence

1. Obtain and read `BJ-DB11-1949-2021` first.
2. If the township sheet is confirmed, prepare a依据卡 for Candidate 1.
3. Keep Candidate 2 as the second Beijing profile.
4. Do not start Candidate 3 until the heritage conservation standard and the legal boundary source are obtained.

This is a sequencing recommendation, not a template decision.
